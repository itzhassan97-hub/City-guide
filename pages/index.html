Why Visit Manchester

A City Built on Invention & Warmth

⚙️ Birthplace of Modern Industry

Walk alongside 18th-century canals, Roman fort ruins in Castlefield, and the world's oldest surviving passenger railway station.

🎵 Music, Art & Football

Experience legendary indie venues, free Pre-Raphaelite galleries, L.S. Lowry's waterfront museum, and world-famous stadium tours.

🚋 Easy Bee Network Travel

Tap-in contactless Metrolink trams and free city-centre buses connect Ancoats, Deansgate, and Salford Quays in minutes.
